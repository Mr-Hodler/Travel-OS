# Research standard

## Research first, write second

Gather every fact before touching the page. Reading the output template before the research is finished anchors the work on document mechanics instead of on what is true.

## What must be checked live, every time

These change and model memory is wrong about them by default:

- who governs a country right now, and the composition of the government
- prices: transport tickets, museum entry, gym day passes, accommodation benchmarks
- opening hours and weekly closing days
- exchange rates, with the rate and its date stated in the page
- entry rules, visa regimes, EES and ETIAS status
- regulatory state: crypto licensing, what is authorised and what is not
- security and geopolitical context
- whether a venue still exists. Restaurants close. A closed restaurant recommended as open is worse than no recommendation
- transport works and seasonal service changes over the travel dates
- seasonal closures and trading-Sunday rules

## What can be written from knowledge

History, geography, culture, customs, food traditions, language notes, why a place is the way it is. These are stable. Research still improves them, but they do not rot.

## Sourcing

Prefer official sources: government portals, embassies, municipal transport operators, national tourist boards, central banks, the venue's own site. A price from the operator beats a price from a travel blog.

When two sources disagree, say so in the page rather than picking silently.

## Trip data comes from the inbox, not from the web

Flights, hotels, confirmations, events and meetings are in email and calendar. Search them. Never reconstruct a booking from memory of the conversation, and never round a price or a confirmation code.

Sources, in order:
1. Spark, all four accounts. `search` with a `newer_than:` filter, `emails` with `from:` filters for a known sender
2. Gmail, for the account it covers, when a Spark result is truncated to a preheader
3. Google Calendar, for meetings, side events and recurring commitments that will collide with travel

Quote codes, times and prices exactly as they appear. A PNR is either right or useless.

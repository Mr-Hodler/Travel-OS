# Travel page skeleton, simplified

The Travel template is the specification and `knowledge/page-standard.md` requires it to be reproduced in full. For a trip that is not complex, a single destination pair, a week or less, no multi-leg intercontinental routing, this is the shape that section list collapses to. Everything below still comes from the template, in the template's order and numbering.

## Order on the page

1. **DA RISOLVERE**, red callout, above section 1. Open items only, one action each.
2. **Voli e transfer.** One row per leg. Carrier, flight number, PNR, route with terminals, date, departure and arrival local times, baggage, price, who paid.
3. **Alloggi.** One row per stay. Property, full address, check-in with time, check-out with time, access method and code, host contact, price, cancellation deadline.
4. **Eventi e impegni fissi.** The single chronological table. Every fixed commitment for the whole trip in one place, across every city, in time order.
5. **Giorno per giorno**, G1 to Gn. One block per day, no gaps.
6. **Giorno libero**, only if the trip has one.
7. **Logistica per città.** Per city: airport to centre, local transport and ticket prices, the SIM or connectivity note, the gym with its day pass price, anything that differs from the City page because of these specific dates.
8. **Checklist.** What to carry, what to do before leaving, what to do on arrival.
9. **Costi.** The cost table and the split.

## The chronological table

| Giorno | Data | Ora | Cosa | Dove | Riferimento | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

One table for the trip. `Giorno` carries the G1 to Gn label so the table and the day-by-day blocks cross-reference. `Riferimento` carries the PNR, booking code or access code verbatim. `Ora` is 24 hour local time at the place the row happens, and where a row crosses a time zone the note says so.

## The cost table

| Voce | Importo | Valuta | CHF | Paga |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

`Paga` is either the employer or him. The total is stated twice, once per payer, never as one mixed figure. State the exchange rate used and the date it was taken.

## Row colours

- **Green** row background on travel rows: flights, trains, transfers, anything where he is in motion.
- **Blue** row background on days that work has taken in full.
- Everything else stays uncoloured. Colour used on more than these two things stops carrying information.

## Day-by-day block

Per day: the fixed commitments pulled from the chronological table, then one suggestion, then the practical note for that day, the check-out time, the bag drop, the thing that has to happen before something closes. A day held entirely by work carries no suggestion. Two hour slots, not half days, because he works remotely and trains every day.

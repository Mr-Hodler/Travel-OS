# Live checks

What gets verified at the source in the 48 hour pass, where from, and what counts as a change worth putting in the action list. Read with `knowledge/research-standard.md`, which sets the general rule: these items change and model memory is wrong about them by default.

## The list

| Item | Source | Flag when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weather on the trip dates | national met office for the destination, not a seasonal average | it changes what goes in the bag, or makes a planned outdoor item pointless |
| Transport strikes | the operator, plus the national strike register where one exists | any action falls on a travel day or a day with a fixed commitment |
| Rail and metro works | the municipal or national operator site | a line in the plan is closed or replaced over the dates |
| Gate and terminal | the airline and the airport departures page | the terminal differs from the confirmation, which is common and never reissued |
| Flight status and schedule change | the airline, by flight number | the time moved at all, even by fifteen minutes, when a transfer is timed to it |
| Opening hours and closing days | the venue's own site | something in the plan is shut on the day it was planned for |
| Public holidays | the country's official calendar | a holiday falls inside the dates, which moves hours, transport and shop opening |
| Venue still exists | the venue's own site or current reviews | it has closed permanently since the page was written |
| Security and entry advisories | the Swiss and Italian foreign ministry pages for the destination | an advisory has been raised, or entry rules changed |
| Entry rules, visa, EES and ETIAS | the official portal for the destination | anything applies that did not when the page was written |
| Exchange rate | the central bank or a rate source, with the date | the cost split in the page was computed at a materially different rate |
| Accommodation still confirmed | the booking platform or the property | the reservation state is not confirmed, or the property has messaged and nobody read it |

## What is not a flag

A forecast that shifts by one degree. A rate that moves under one percent. An advisory reworded with no change in substance. A gate number that is not published yet, which is normal until the day. Reporting these trains him to skim the list, and the list only works if every line on it needs an action.

## Sourcing discipline

Official over aggregated, every time: the airport over a flight tracker, the operator over a news article, the ministry over a travel blog. When two sources disagree, put both in the action list and say they disagree rather than picking one silently.

Nothing on this list is answered from memory. A price, an hour and an advisory recalled rather than checked is the failure mode this whole pass exists to prevent.
